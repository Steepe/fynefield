Our story

Incorporated on 31 January 2013, Fynefield is a Nigerian, vertically integrated downstream company.

Our operations span petroleum product trading, bulk storage, haulage and distribution, and retail outlets.

We bring disciplined operations and customer focus to every link in the downstream value chain — so product is where it needs to be, when it needs to be there.
